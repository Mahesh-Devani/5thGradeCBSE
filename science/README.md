# 🔬 Science Master — Class 5 CBSE (EVS Curriculum)

An interactive, discovery-based Science learning studio for **CBSE Class 5 students (Ages 9–11)**. Built with heavy weightage on **CBSE school revision worksheets & answer keys** and the official NCERT / CBSE curriculum, fostering **deductive reasoning, scientific thinking, and conceptual clarity** rather than rote memorization.

---

## 🎯 Pedagogical Philosophy: The 4 Thinking Pillars

1. **Unforgettable Mental Metaphors & Visual Labs**:
   - *The Scuba Diver vs Whale*: Why marine mammals have blowholes on top of their head and breathe air with lungs.
   - *The Emergency Power Bank*: Why Shama is fine after a 2-day fast (liver glycogen and adipose fat reserves).
   - *The Invisible Weight Lifter*: Tina's balloon balance proving air has mass and weight.
   - *The Molecular Dance Floor*: Interactive visualizer for solids, liquids, and gases.
   - *The Coffee Scent Rocket*: How thermal kinetic energy accelerates molecular diffusion.
   - *The Tale of Two Hooks*: Why an iron hook rusts in humid Kerala but survives in dry Rajasthan.
2. **Metacognitive Self-Talk (`💡 Think About It` Prompts)**:
   - Thought-provoking questions that guide students on *how to reason* through scientific problems before answering.
3. **Diagnostic "Spot the Exam Trap / Be the Teacher"**:
   - Students review sample student test slips (Rohan, Priya, Sneha, Aditya, etc.) to catch subtle misconceptions (e.g. attempting to filter dissolved salt with filter paper, assuming milk provides 100% of all nutrients, confusing nares with movement limbs).
4. **Interactive Practice Drills Matching School Worksheets**:
   - Filterable categories: **MCQs & Deductions**, **Who Am I? Riddles**, **Compare & Contrast (Venn/Table)**, **Odd One Out with Justification**, and **Real-World Scenarios / Case Studies** (Gerry & Freddy, Tim's Aquarium, Danny's Rickets, Anu's IT Morning Breakfast).

## 🛠️ Modes & Features

1. **📖 Learn & Explore**:
   - Visual concept cards with bite-sized takeaways.
   - Interactive gadgets: **Molecular Agitator**, **Tina’s Balloon Balance Simulator**, **Nutrient Inspector**, and **Animal Adaptation Blueprint**.
   - Metacognitive `💡 Think About It` prompts.
2. **🕵️ Spot Traps (Be the Teacher)**:
   - Diagnostic simulated test slips exposing subtle 5th-grade exam pitfalls.
3. **🧩 Practice & Worksheets**:
   - Filterable drills: **MCQs**, **Who Am I?**, **Compare & Contrast**, **Odd One Out**, and **Case Studies**.
   - Immediate feedback cards with scientific takeaways.
   - In-progress quiz session persistence across browser reloads.
   - Prominent `🔄 Restart Drill` button.
4. **⚡ 60-Second Science Sprint**:
   - Rapid-fire conceptual sprint with timer, audio effects, and star rewards (1–3 stars).
5. **📄 Printable Worksheet Studio**:
   - Standard A4 black-and-white exam paper layout matching school assessments.
   - Toggle between **Student Test Mode** (blank response lines) and **Teacher Answer Key Mode** (with grading rubric).

## 📱 Technical Standards

- **Zero External Dependencies**: 100% vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and ES6+ JavaScript.
- **Audio Feedback**: Native Web Audio API (`AudioContext`) oscillator synthesizer (no external sound files).
- **Celebratory Confetti**: Native HTML5 Canvas particle system.
- **State & Session Persistence**: `localStorage` key `cbse5_science_active_state` and `cbse5_science_stars_*`.
- **Mobile First**: Sliding drawer on screens $\le 860\text{px}$, 44px+ touch targets, `touch-action: manipulation;`.
- **CSS Brace Balance**: Verified `Open braces: 0`.
